How rules are evaluated Details

A condition must hold for two minutes to open an incident, and must clear for two minutes to recover.

Checker metrics use a 15-minute window with at least 20 samples. Missing data keeps an open incident open.

Usable counts follow the rotator's filters. Success rate and latency cover workspace checks for its upstream protocol over TCP, including failed and subsequently paused routes. Rotators sharing a protocol share these checker metrics.
